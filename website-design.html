How long does it take to build a website?

Most small-business websites take 1 to 6 weeks. With BunkWise, a Starter site takes about 1 week, Business about 2 weeks, Growth about 3 weeks and Premium 4–6 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly your content and photos are ready.

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram or a Google Business Profile?

Yes, if you want to be found and trusted. Social profiles and listings are space you rent; a website is the one place you control. It ranks for searches a profile can't, gives AI assistants a reliable source to describe your business, and is where your Google profile and ads should send people.

Read: Do I need a website for my business? →

Should I choose a custom website or a Wix, Squarespace or WordPress template?

Templates are quick to start but are shared by thousands of sites, often load slowly on mobile and are harder to rank. A custom, hand-built website is designed around your customers, loads faster and is structured for search from day one. For a business that depends on enquiries, the custom route usually pays for itself.

Read: Website builder vs freelancer vs agency →

How do I get my business on top of Google search?

Combine a fast website that targets the exact phrases your customers search, a complete Google Business Profile with regular reviews, the same business details across directories, and helpful content added over time. There is no instant route; results compound month by month.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT and other AI assistants?

AI assistants recommend businesses they can clearly understand and verify. That means a website that states plainly who you are, what you offer and where; question-and-answer content that matches what people ask; structured data; and consistent business details across Google, directories and reviews. Nobody can guarantee a mention, but these steps are the foundation.

Read: How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT →

What is AEO and GEO, and how are they different from SEO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) helps your pages rank in Google's results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) shapes your content into clear questions and answers that search features and voice assistants can quote. GEO (generative engine optimisation) makes your business information clear and consistent so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand and cite it. A website that wants customers in 2026 needs all three.

How much does SEO cost per month?

Local SEO for a small business typically costs $500–$3,000 a month in the US. BunkWise monthly SEO plans start at ₹7,999 in India and $499 for international clients, and include Google Business Profile optimisation, technical fixes and a monthly report.

Is it worth paying someone to build a website?

If the website is meant to bring in customers, usually yes. DIY builders cost less to start but take your time and rarely rank or convert well without design and SEO experience. For many businesses, one or two extra customers a month is enough to cover the cost of a professional site.
