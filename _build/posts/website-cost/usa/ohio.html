Ohio's tax rule and other local specifics

Computer services can be taxable

Ohio's sales tax reaches some computer-related services. Under Ohio Administrative Code 5703-9-46, automatic data processing, computer services (which include computer programming) and electronic information services are taxable when bought for use in a business and when they are the "true object" of the transaction. The rule doesn't mention website design by name. Whether a particular website contract is taxable can therefore depend on how the work is scoped and invoiced, so ask your provider how they treat it and check with your accountant.

Monthly website plans are common

Some Ohio agencies offer websites as a subscription. Columbus Web SEO, for example, sells plans at $100–$500 a month with a setup fee of $0–$2,500, and TL Web Design mentions managed monthly plans alongside one-time builds. A plan spreads the cost and bundles support and hosting. The trade-off is total cost over time and ownership: before signing, ask who owns the design and content if you cancel, and add up three years of payments.

Privacy

Ohio does not have a comprehensive consumer privacy law in force, according to the IAPP's state tracker. A clear privacy policy and sensible handling of form submissions are still good practice for any business website.

Running costs after launch

  • Hosting: $10–$50 a month for a small business, sometimes bundled into a plan (Columbus Web SEO).
  • Domain: $15–$25 a year.
  • Maintenance: $50–$300 a month, depending on whether you or someone else manages updates. TL Web Design quotes $99–$199 a month for hosting and maintenance together.
  • SEO: a real ongoing programme generally starts around $500 a month in Columbus.

Columbus Web SEO warns against $99-a-month "autopilot" SEO offers. If an SEO plan can't tell you what work is done each month, it probably isn't doing much.

How to compare Ohio quotes

  1. Decide whether you need a brochure site or a lead-generation site, and compare quotes for the same kind.
  2. Ask whether any Ohio sales tax will be charged, and on which parts of the invoice.
  3. For monthly plans, total three years of payments and check ownership on exit.
  4. Ask whether service-area pages, schema markup and call tracking are included.
  5. Confirm the domain is registered to your business.
  6. Ask for two or three live sites for businesses like yours.

Hiring outside Ohio

Ohio is on Eastern Time, so India is 9.5 hours ahead in summer and 10.5 in winter. With a designer there, the project runs mostly in writing: you send notes during the day, changes are made overnight, and calls fit early in your morning.
