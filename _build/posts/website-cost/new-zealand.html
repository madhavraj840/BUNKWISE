What different NZ businesses usually need

The right budget depends less on your industry than on what the site has to do. Some common patterns:

  • Tradies (builders, electricians, plumbers) get most value from a page for each service and each area they cover, a quote request form, and photos of real jobs. That is usually a lead-generation site, which Horton Taylor prices at NZ$2,500–NZ$4,500 plus GST.
  • Cafés and restaurants need an up-to-date menu, opening hours, a booking link and a well-maintained Google Business Profile more than they need many pages. A template site can do the job if it is kept current.
  • Professional firms (accountants, lawyers, consultants) are judged on credibility, so clear service pages, team profiles and case studies matter, which pushes them towards the 8–15 page professional sites Fuel Design prices at NZ$5,990–NZ$15,000.
  • Online retailers should budget for e-commerce from NZ$4,990 (Fuel Design), plus integrations such as accounting and shipping.

GST, subscriptions and privacy in New Zealand

GST at 15%, usually on top

New Zealand's GST rate is 15%, and most NZ studios quote prices plus GST, as Horton Taylor points out. If your business is GST-registered, you can normally claim it back; if not, add 15% to any "plus GST" quote before comparing it with one that already includes tax. The difference on an NZ$8,000 build is NZ$1,200.

Subscription websites: cheaper to start, not always cheaper overall

Alpine Studio lists boutique studios charging NZ$499–NZ$1,999 to set up a site, then NZ$79–NZ$199 a month. That model spreads the cost and usually bundles hosting, updates and support. The trade-offs are worth checking before you sign:

  • Total cost: at NZ$129 a month, three years of fees come to NZ$4,644, on top of the setup fee.
  • Ownership: ask whether you own the design, content and domain, and what happens to the site if you cancel.
  • Minimum term: check the contract length and exit terms.

For a business that wants predictable monthly costs and hands-off support, a subscription can be the right choice. For one that plans to keep its site for years, an outright build is often cheaper.

The Privacy Act 2020

Any NZ website that collects personal information through an enquiry or booking form is covered by the Privacy Act 2020 and its information privacy principles, which include telling people when their information is being collected and why, and keeping it secure. In practice that means a privacy statement, clear wording on forms and sensible handling of what the forms collect.

Auckland versus the regions

Alpine Studio reports that Auckland agencies charge 30–50% more than regional studios, and puts Queenstown agencies at NZ$3,000–NZ$10,000+ for a full build. Developer rates for changes run NZ$80–NZ$150 an hour.

Running costs after launch

  • Hosting: NZ$39–NZ$99+ a month for managed hosting (Fuel Design, Horton Taylor)
  • Domain: NZ$16.50–NZ$40 a year for a .co.nz (Horton Taylor), NZ$25–NZ$65 according to Alpine Studio
  • Maintenance: NZ$50–NZ$200 a month (Fuel Design)
  • Plugin licences: NZ$200–NZ$800 a year (Alpine Studio)
  • SEO: NZ$500–NZ$3,000+ a month if you want ongoing work (Fuel Design)

How to compare NZ quotes

  1. Check whether each quote is plus or including GST.
  2. For subscriptions, add up the total over three years and check ownership and exit terms.
  3. Ask for the page list and features in writing.
  4. Make sure the .co.nz or .nz domain is registered to your business.
  5. Ask what "SEO" includes, and who writes the page text.
  6. Ask what changes cost after launch.

Working with a designer outside New Zealand

New Zealand is 6.5 hours ahead of India (7.5 during NZ daylight saving), so a New Zealand afternoon overlaps with the Indian morning. Feedback sent in your afternoon can be worked on the same day, and calls fit best after lunch in New Zealand. Fuel Design lists offshore teams at NZ$500–NZ$5,000, a range that spans everything from templates to properly scoped builds, so the checks matter: live work, a written scope, milestone payments and all accounts in your name.
