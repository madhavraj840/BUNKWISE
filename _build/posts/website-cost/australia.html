Quick answer

Local freelancers typically charge A$1,500–A$2,500+, small agencies A$3,000–A$7,000+ and creative agencies A$10,000–A$50,000+ (Paramark). Hosting and a care plan add roughly A$65–A$450 a month. Australia's web design market is unusually spread out. At one end are offshore freelancers selling sites for a few hundred dollars; at the other are Sydney and Melbourne creative agencies whose projects start at five figures. The difference between the two is not only price: it is how much strategy, copywriting and search work sits behind the design. Prices by type of website

What's The Damage, which says it cross-references more than 90 Australian pricing sources and was last checked in September 2026, breaks prices down by the kind of site:

  • Landing page: A$800–A$3,000
  • Small business site: A$2,000–A$6,000
  • Custom build: A$6,000–A$15,000
  • Larger site with integrations: A$15,000–A$40,000
  • E-commerce: A$10,000–A$60,000+

Spark Interact's guide sits higher, at A$3,000–A$8,000 for brochure sites and A$5,000–A$15,000 for small business websites. Paramark notes that a custom build usually takes 30 to 60 days, while a template site can be live in a few days.

What changes between a A$2,000 and a A$6,000 website

Both ends of the typical range can produce a site that looks professional on launch day. The difference shows up in the months after, in how often the site brings in an enquiry. At the lower end you usually get a theme adapted to your colours, the text you supply, and basic settings such as page titles. Towards the upper end you should expect:

  • Research before design: which services and suburbs people actually search for, and what the businesses ranking above you do on their sites.
  • A page for each main service and, for trades and clinics, the areas you cover, so the site can rank for more than just your business name.
  • Copy written for search and for conversion, not only for appearance.
  • Structured data and a connected Google Business Profile, so search engines and AI assistants understand what you do and where.
  • Speed work on mobile phones, where a slow page loses visitors before it finishes loading.

If your site only needs to confirm you exist when someone already has your name, the lower end is fine. If it needs to find new customers, the extra work is where the value is.

GST, privacy and city differences

GST: 10%, but not on every quote

GST is 10% on most services, but a business only has to register once its GST turnover reaches A$75,000. Many sole-trader web designers are below that and don't charge GST, while agencies do. If your own business isn't registered, a 10% difference between otherwise identical quotes is real money, so compare them on the same basis.

The Privacy Act applies to some small businesses too

According to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the Privacy Act generally applies to businesses with annual turnover above A$3 million. It also covers some smaller businesses regardless of turnover, including private health service providers. For a clinic, physio or dental practice, the website's contact and booking forms, privacy policy and data storage should be set up with that in mind.

Sydney costs more; regional Australia costs less

What's The Damage shows the same A$2,000–A$6,000 typical range in every capital city, with prices rising 3.2–3.8% year on year (fastest in Sydney). Hourly rates vary more. Spark Interact reports a 20–30% premium for Sydney designers over regional ones, with typical rates of:

  • Melbourne: A$150–A$200 an hour
  • Sydney: around A$120 an hour
  • Perth and Adelaide: A$100–A$150 an hour
  • Brisbane: around A$90 an hour
  • Regional areas: A$50–A$100 an hour

Across the country, What's The Damage puts freelancers at A$80–A$150 an hour and agencies at A$100–A$250.

Running costs after launch

  • Hosting: A$15–A$150 a month (What's The Damage)
  • Care plan or maintenance: A$50–A$300 a month
  • Plugins and licences: A$0–A$100 a month; Paramark puts essential plugins at A$100–A$300 a year
  • .com.au domain: A$15–A$30 a year
  • Photography: around A$1,500 for a half-day shoot (Paramark)

Some Australian providers bundle the build into a monthly subscription. That lowers the upfront cost, but check who owns the site if you stop paying, and add up what you would pay over three years.

How to compare Australian quotes

  1. Check whether each quote includes GST.
  2. Ask for the page list and features in writing, and whether the price is fixed.
  3. Confirm the .com.au domain is registered to your business's ABN, not to the designer.
  4. Ask whether the build is custom or based on a template, and who writes the text.
  5. If you run a health or allied-health business, ask how enquiry and booking data will be handled.
  6. Ask what the monthly fee covers and what leaving would involve.

Working with a designer outside Australia

The time difference between Australia and India is smaller than most people expect. Sydney and Melbourne are 4.5 hours ahead of India (5.5 during daylight saving), Brisbane is 4.5 hours ahead all year, and Perth only 2.5 hours. An Australian afternoon overlaps the Indian working morning, so same-day feedback is realistic.

Paramark's figures show why offshore work has a mixed reputation in Australia: at A$100–A$500, a site is usually a lightly edited template with little support.
