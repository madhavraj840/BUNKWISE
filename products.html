Night Mode

In testing. Public release on 30 September 2026.

Dims your screen below the lowest brightness Android allows, so the phone is comfortable to use in a dark room. It works entirely on your phone: no account, no data collection and no ads.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps (required) — draws the dark layer over the screen.
  • Accessibility (optional) — turns off dimming with the volume buttons, and applies different dimming per app.
