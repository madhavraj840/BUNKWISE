Quick answer

In 2026, most Texas small businesses budget $3,000–$7,000 for a professional website set up for local search. In Houston, HTX Web Designs prices a small four-to-six page site at $1,500–$3,500 and a custom build with local search work at $3,500–$7,000; in San Antonio, local agencies typically charge $3,000–$6,000 for five to seven pages, according to Breck AI Media. Unusually, Texas treats website creation as a taxable service, taxing 80% of the price. BunkWise's packages start at $999.

Texas has a big, competitive web design market spread across four major metros, and pricing reflects that competition: flat-fee packages under $1,000 sit alongside agency builds well into five figures. What really separates Texas from most other states is tax. Where most states treat website design as an untaxed service, Texas generally taxes it. That changes the real cost of a quote, and it is worth understanding before you compare prices.

Houston website prices

HTX Web Designs, a Houston studio, splits the local market by budget:

  • $1,500–$3,500 buys a tidy site of four to six pages, suited to sole traders and new businesses.
  • $3,500–$7,000 covers a fully custom build with local search work, pages for the areas you serve, enquiry forms and a content system you can edit. HTX Web Designs sees this as where most Houston small businesses should aim.
  • $7,000–$15,000 and up is for online shops, bookings, several locations or sites of more than twenty pages.

By provider, the same guide lists DIY builders at $25–$500 a month, freelancers at $500–$5,000 and local agencies at $2,000–$15,000+, and says most small business websites fall between $2,000 and $10,000.

Texas taxes website creation

This is the part most out-of-state guides miss. In a 2024 letter ruling (No. 202404008L), the Texas Comptroller treated the creation and maintenance of a website as a data processing service, which is taxable in Texas. The Sales Tax Institute's summary quotes the reasoning: building and maintaining a site "involves the compilation, storage, and manipulation of data." Pure planning or design blueprints and consulting were treated as non-taxable.

There is a partial break: under Section 151.351 of the Texas Tax Code, 20% of the value of data processing services is exempt, so sales tax applies to 80% of the price. On a $5,000 website, tax is charged on $4,000. When you compare quotes, check whether a Texas provider has added this tax, and whether a lower quote simply leaves it out.

Privacy: the TDPSA exempts most small businesses

The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act took effect on July 1, 2024. As Davis Wright Tremaine explains, it has no revenue threshold; instead it exempts businesses that meet the SBA's definition of a small business, except that even they must get consumer consent before selling sensitive personal data. For most local service businesses, a clear privacy policy and sensible form handling are what the website needs.

Running costs after launch

  • Hosting, security and support: $100–$500 a month according to both HTX Web Designs and North Texas Web Design.
  • Domain: $10–$60 a year depending on the extension (Southtown Designs).
  • Shared hosting: about $33 a month in Southtown's example, rising for VPS or cloud hosting.
  • Copywriting: $50–$500 a page if you don't write it yourself.

HTX Web Designs makes a fair point: a $1,000 site that never ranks can cost more than a $4,000 site that brings in work. Compare what each quote is designed to achieve, not only its price.

How to compare Texas quotes

  1. Ask whether Texas sales tax is included, and on what share of the price.
  2. Get a written list of pages and features, with a fixed price.
  3. Ask whether service-area pages are included if you cover several suburbs or cities.
  4. Check that you will own the domain, hosting account and the finished site.
  5. Ask what "SEO included" covers and how Google Business Profile will be set up.
  6. Ask what maintenance costs after launch, and what it includes.

Hiring a designer outside Texas

Texas is on Central Time, so India is 10.5 hours ahead in summer and 11.5 in winter (El Paso, on Mountain Time, is an hour further back). Work with a designer in India tends to run overnight: you review in the afternoon, and changes are ready the next morning. Calls fit best early in the Texas morning.

HTX Web Designs warns that some overseas providers charge $300–$2,500 and know nothing about the Houston market, which is a fair caution about the cheapest end of any market, local or overseas. Whoever you hire, judge them on live work and a written scope. Also note the tax point: an overseas provider won't normally collect Texas sales tax, but Texas use tax rules may still apply to your purchase, so ask your accountant. Frequently asked questions

How much does a small business website cost in Texas?

In Houston, HTX Web Designs puts a clean 4–6 page site at $1,500–$3,500 and a fully custom site with local SEO at $3,500–$7,000. In San Antonio, Breck AI Media says local agencies typically charge $3,000–$6,000 for five to seven pages.

Is website design taxable in Texas?

Creating and maintaining a website is generally taxable in Texas as a data processing service, according to a 2024 Comptroller letter ruling summarised by the Sales Tax Institute. Texas taxes 80% of the price of data processing services, because the Tax Code exempts 20%. Planning-only design work and consulting are treated differently.

How much do Texas web designers charge per hour?

Freelancers in San Antonio typically charge $75–$150 an hour, according to Breck AI Media, and Southtown Designs puts developer time for updates at $90–$150 an hour.

Does the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act apply to my small business?

Usually not. The TDPSA took effect on July 1, 2024, and exempts businesses that meet the SBA's small-business definition, except that they must get consent before selling sensitive personal data.

How much does it cost to maintain a website in Texas?

Houston and North Texas agencies both quote $100–$500 a month for hosting, security updates and support, depending on how much work is included.
