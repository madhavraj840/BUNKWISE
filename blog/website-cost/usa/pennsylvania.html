Quick answer

In 2026, a professional 5–10 page small business website in Pennsylvania typically costs $1,200–$4,000, according to Sleek Website Design, while agency projects commonly run $3,500–$12,000 (WebTek). Unlike most states, Pennsylvania generally treats website design and website maintenance as taxable, at 6% plus local tax in Allegheny County and Philadelphia. BunkWise's packages start at $999.

Pennsylvania's web design market runs from freelancers in smaller towns building sites for a few hundred dollars to Philadelphia and Pittsburgh agencies whose projects start in five figures. The prices themselves are close to the national picture. What makes Pennsylvania different is tax: it is one of the few states where the Department of Revenue treats website design as a taxable sale, and where ongoing maintenance is taxable too. That affects the real cost of every quote.

The same guide notes that a quality five-page site usually lands between $1,200 and $4,000, depending on how custom the design is and whether copywriting and SEO setup are included.

Agency pricing in Pennsylvania

WebTek, a Pennsylvania agency writing in July 2026, starts from the full spread (a professional website can cost anywhere from a few hundred dollars to $50,000 or more) and then describes the upper market: for most small to mid-sized businesses, a professionally built website "often falls between $8,500 and $35,000", with e-commerce, custom tools and large content builds going higher. Most of its own projects fall somewhere from $3,000 to $20,000, commonly $3,500–$12,000. WebTek's hosting starts at $39.95 a month and domains at around $29.95 a year.

Between the two sources, a Pennsylvania small business has a clear choice: a freelancer or small studio in the $1,200–$4,000 band for a well-built five to ten page site, or an agency engagement from about $3,500 when the project needs strategy, content and more pages.

Pennsylvania taxes website design

This is the key difference from neighbouring states such as New York and New Jersey. The Pennsylvania Department of Revenue treats website design as taxable when the website is transferred to the customer, and it treats both the website and its maintenance as subject to sales and use tax. The state rate is 6%, with an additional 1% in Allegheny County (Pittsburgh) and 2% in Philadelphia.

In practice, that means:

  • A $4,000 website from a Pennsylvania provider may carry $240–$320 in sales tax.
  • Monthly maintenance plans are taxable too, which adds up over several years.
  • When comparing quotes, check whether tax is included, and whether a cheaper quote simply leaves it out.
  • If you buy from a provider outside the state who doesn't charge Pennsylvania tax, use tax may still be due on your side. Your accountant can confirm.

Accessibility lawsuits

Pennsylvania had 137 federal website accessibility lawsuits in 2025, the fifth-highest of any state, according to Seyfarth Shaw. That is far fewer than New York or Florida, but enough to make accessibility a sensible part of the brief for any business with premises open to the public.

Privacy

Pennsylvania does not yet have a comprehensive consumer privacy law of the kind in force in about twenty states, according to the IAPP's state privacy tracker. A clear privacy policy and sensible handling of contact form data are still expected.

Running costs after launch

  • Hosting: from about $39.95 a month through an agency such as WebTek; cheaper shared hosting is available elsewhere.
  • Domain: from about $29.95 a year through WebTek; standard .com registrations elsewhere can cost less.
  • Maintenance: priced per plan, and taxable in Pennsylvania.
  • Content updates and SEO: optional, but they are what keeps a site improving after launch.

Because maintenance is taxable in Pennsylvania, a monthly plan's real cost is its price plus 6–8%. Factor that into any comparison between a maintained site and one you update yourself.

How to compare Pennsylvania quotes

  1. Ask whether sales tax is included on the build and on maintenance.
  2. Get a written page list and whether the design is custom or template-based.
  3. Ask who writes the text and whether SEO setup is included.
  4. Confirm the domain and hosting are registered to your business.
  5. Ask what changes cost after launch, and whether maintenance is optional.
  6. Open two or three live sites the provider built for similar businesses.

Hiring outside Pennsylvania

India is 9.5 hours ahead of Pennsylvania in summer and 10.5 in winter. With a designer there, review sessions work best early in your morning, and changes tend to be made while you sleep. Most of the project runs in writing, which also creates a useful record of what was agreed.

Tax is the Pennsylvania-specific point to raise: an overseas designer won't collect Pennsylvania sales tax, so ask your accountant whether use tax applies to your purchase. Frequently asked questions

How much does a small business website cost in Pennsylvania?

A professional 5–10 page small business site typically costs $1,200–$4,000, according to Sleek Website Design's 2026 Pennsylvania guide, with starter sites of 1–3 pages at $300–$1,200 and advanced or e-commerce builds at $4,000–$12,000+. Agency projects commonly run $3,500–$12,000.

Is website design taxable in Pennsylvania?

Yes, in many cases. The Pennsylvania Department of Revenue treats website design as taxable when the website is transferred to the customer, and website maintenance is taxable too. Pennsylvania's sales tax is 6%, with an extra 1% in Allegheny County and 2% in Philadelphia.

Why are Philadelphia websites more expensive?

Agencies in larger metros carry higher overheads and often work with bigger clients. WebTek, a Pennsylvania agency, says professionally built sites for small to mid-sized businesses often fall between $8,500 and $35,000, while its own projects commonly run $3,500–$12,000.

Are website accessibility lawsuits filed in Pennsylvania?

Yes. Pennsylvania had 137 federal website accessibility lawsuits in 2025, the fifth-highest of any state, according to law firm Seyfarth Shaw.

How much does hosting a business website cost in Pennsylvania?

Hosting from a Pennsylvania agency such as WebTek starts around $39.95 a month, while basic shared hosting elsewhere costs less. Remember that website maintenance services are taxable in Pennsylvania.
