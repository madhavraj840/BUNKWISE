Website Builder vs Freelancer vs Agency: Which Is Best for a Small Business?

BunkWise Team Sep 28, 2026 7 min read
  • website builder
  • freelancer
  • web agency
  • custom website
  • hiring

Three ways to get a website.

Each one fits a different business.

BuilderDIY Freelancercustom Agencyfull team

Quick answer

Use a website builder such as Wix or Squarespace if your budget is very small and you have time to do it yourself. Hire a freelancer or small studio for a custom site at a moderate cost, as long as you can check their live work. Choose an agency for large or complex projects that need a whole team. For most small businesses that need enquiries from Google, a proven freelancer or small studio is the best balance of cost and quality.

Website builders (Wix, Squarespace and similar)

Good for: getting online quickly on a tiny budget, when you're comfortable doing it yourself.

  • Pros: low upfront cost, quick to start, no code needed.
  • Cons: your time adds up, templates are shared by many sites, and you have limited control over speed and structure, which matters for SEO in competitive markets.

Freelancers and small studios

Good for: small businesses that want a custom website built around their customers, without agency prices.

  • Pros: custom design, direct communication, flexible, usually far cheaper than an agency.
  • Cons: quality varies widely, so checking live work matters. A single freelancer can also be a bottleneck when they are busy.

Agencies

Good for: larger projects that need design, development, content, marketing and project management together.

  • Pros: a full team, broad services, established processes.
  • Cons: the highest cost, and small businesses can end up a low priority among bigger clients.

Questions to ask before you hire anyone

  1. Can I see live websites you've built for businesses like mine?
  2. What exactly is included: pages, content writing, SEO, launch setup?
  3. How long will it take, and what do you need from me?
  4. How will the site perform on a phone?
  5. What happens after launch if I need changes?
  6. Are your prices written down before we start?

Frequently asked questions

Is a website builder like Wix good enough for SEO?

A website builder can rank for simple, low-competition local searches when it is set up carefully. You have less control over speed, code and structure, so in competitive markets a custom-built site usually has an advantage.

Is it cheaper to hire a freelancer or an agency?

A freelancer is usually cheaper. In the US, freelancers typically charge $500–$5,000 for a small business website, while agencies typically charge $5,000–$15,000. Agencies cost more because they bring a team, project management and broader services.

How do I check a web designer's work before hiring?

Ask for links to live websites, not just screenshots. Open them on your phone, check how fast they load, and search Google for a couple of those businesses to see whether their sites actually appear.
