Night mode

Live · Windows and Android

A screen dimmer that keeps going after the brightness slider stops. On a laptop it lowers the real backlight first, then dims further on screen, down to 4% of normal brightness.

  • Free for Windows 10 and 11, with open-source code on GitHub (MIT)
  • Android version live on Google Play
  • No account, no ads, works offline

About BunkWise technology

What technology does BunkWise build?

BunkWise Technologies designs and builds websites for businesses in India and worldwide. It also makes its own software: Night mode, a screen dimmer for Windows and Android, a QR scanner app, and free attendance, SGPA and CGPA calculators used by 13,000+ people a month.

Is Night mode free and open source?

Yes. Night mode for Windows is free, and its full source code is on GitHub under the MIT licence. The Android version is free on Google Play, with optional Pro features.
