What we're building towards

Technology is where we started. These are the fields we plan to enter next, one at a time.

  1. 01

    Technology

    Apps and software we design, build and run ourselves, and website design for businesses worldwide. This is where BunkWise began, and it supports everything else we do.

    Active
  2. 02

    Hyperlocal delivery

    A hyperlocal delivery platform designed for residential communities, providing convenient and rapid access to fresh produce and daily essentials from nearby suppliers while reducing the higher fees typically associated with large quick-commerce platforms.

    In progress
  3. 03

    Agriculture

    Establishing sustainable vermicomposting facilities that convert organic waste into high-quality, nutrient-rich natural fertiliser for agricultural farms, gardens, and horticultural applications.

    Planned
  4. 04

    Cement processing

    Processing cement and loading it into bulk cement tankers for construction sites. We process and supply; we don't manufacture.

    Planned
  5. 05

    Challenging traditional diagnostics, which tend to focus on the disease rather than its root cause. We plan to build systems that help identify the root cause, working in medical innovation and medical technology (MedTech).

    Planned
  6. 06

    Fund management

    Growing, over the long term, into managing investments across global markets.

    Long term
