How we work

The habits that came from building our own products

  1. 01

    Start with the real problem

    Our first product began with a question students kept asking: can I skip this class? We built the answer, not a feature list. Client websites start the same way, with how your customers search and what makes them call.

  2. 02

    Ship, then measure

    Launch day is when the learning starts. Every website we hand over has Analytics and Search Console set up, so the next decision comes from numbers rather than guesses.

  3. 03

    One person accountable

    You talk to the person who builds your project, from the first message to launch. Nothing gets lost between a salesperson, a designer and a developer.

  4. 04

    Prices in public

    Our website packages and monthly plans are published for nine regions. You know what things cost before you ask us.

  5. 05

    Proof you can check

    Every client site we list is live. Open them on your phone, see how fast they load, and judge the work before you spend anything.

Founder

Madhav

Founder & Developer

I'm a final-year engineering student in India. I started BunkWise to save students five minutes of mental maths before deciding whether to skip a lecture. I built the calculation engine, the subject-wise breakdown and the interface myself, and thousands of students now use it every month.

Building it taught me what our clients get today: start with the real problem, make something fast and clear, put it in front of people and keep improving it. That's how we design websites for businesses, and it's how BunkWise plans to grow into the sectors on our roadmap.
