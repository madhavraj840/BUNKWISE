1Offline-first approach

Night mode is designed to run entirely on your device. Turning the dimming overlay on or off, choosing a dim level, scheduling bedtime hours, setting per-app profiles, saving presets, and every other feature are all processed locally. We do not operate any servers that collect, store, process, sell, rent, or share your settings, your usage, or anything about how you use the App.

2Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from users. Specifically, we do not collect:

The App has no account system and no sign-in of any kind - there is nothing tied to you for us to collect even if we wanted to.

3Settings stored on your device

The App stores your preferences locally, using Android's standard app-storage mechanism, so they persist between sessions:

  • Dimming on/off state and dim level
  • Schedule on/off times and repeat days
  • Per-app profile list and levels
  • Saved presets
  • Color filter/tint choice
  • Notification and Quick Settings tile preferences
  • Local usage statistics (total time dimmed, session count) shown back to you in the App

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

4Accessibility Service - what it sees, and why

Emergency off
Watches for the Volume Up + Volume Down button combination, so you can turn dimming off from anywhere, even outside the App. It does not read anything else about what you're doing.
Per-app profiles
Reads which app is currently in the foreground, purely to apply the dim level you've chosen for that app. The current app's identity is used only in memory to pick a level - it is never logged, stored as history, or transmitted.
Auto-Dim Pro, optional
Periodically captures a screenshot through Android's Accessibility screenshot API, shrinks it to a tiny low-resolution grid, and measures its average brightness on-device to nudge the dim level automatically. The screenshot is processed entirely in memory and discarded immediately after - never saved as an image, never written to disk, never transmitted anywhere. Auto-Dim only runs while you've explicitly turned it on.

All three share one Accessibility service, but each has its own on/off switch, so turning one on doesn't turn on the others - and none of them function unless you've separately granted Accessibility permission in Android's system settings.

5Permissions used by the App

Display over other apps
Draws the darkening overlay itself - the one permission the App's core function depends on.
Notifications
Shows an ongoing status notification while dimming is on, with optional quick-adjust buttons.
Accessibility optional
Powers the three features described in Section 4, and nothing else.
Ignore battery optimization optional
Some phone manufacturers aggressively stop background apps to save power; this helps keep dimming running reliably. Asked for, never required.
Run at startup
Lets the App restore your schedule and, if you've turned that on, resume dimming after your phone restarts.
Vibration
Haptic feedback on certain interactions.
Foreground service
Android requires an ongoing, visible service (with a notification) for an app like this to keep the overlay active reliably - that's what this provides.

6No internet access

The App does not request Android's Internet permission at all. It has no technical ability to send data anywhere - over Wi-Fi, mobile data, or otherwise.

7Data sharing and third parties

Because we do not collect personal information, and the App has no internet access, we do not - and cannot - sell, rent, share, trade, or disclose personal information to third parties. The App contains no advertising and no analytics software of any kind.

8Data security

Since nothing is transmitted to or stored on any server we operate, there is no remote data of yours to secure. Information stored by the App remains on your device; keeping your device itself secure (screen lock, OS updates, and so on) is what protects it.

9Data retention and deletion

We do not retain personal information because we do not collect it. Data stored locally by the App remains entirely under your control. You can delete it by:

  • Using "Reset everything" in the App's Diagnostics screen
  • Clearing the App's storage through Android Settings
  • Uninstalling the App - since there's no account and no server, uninstalling removes everything the App stored, with nothing left behind anywhere

10Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

11Third-party links

The App does not currently link out to any external websites or services from within its own screens. If that changes in a future version, this section will be updated to reflect it.

12Open source software

The App is built using standard Android and Jetpack Compose framework libraries, each under its own open-source license (for example, the Apache License 2.0). No proprietary third-party SDKs are used.
